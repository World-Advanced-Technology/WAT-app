Our Story

Pioneering Technology Excellence in Cameroon

World Advanced Technology (WAT) is Cameroon's premier IT solutions company, founded with a singular vision: to bring enterprise-grade technology to African businesses at accessible prices. Based in Yaoundé, we serve clients across Cameroon and beyond.

Led by CEO NGWA CLOVIS OCHANG, a Cisco-certified network engineer and AWS Cloud professional, our team delivers cutting-edge DevOps infrastructure, private VoIP telephone systems, and comprehensive IT consultancy services that transform how businesses operate.

Our VoIP solutions are trusted by banks, microfinance institutions, and multi-branch companies across the country, enabling seamless, cost-effective communication. We are supported by a professional, well-trained staff committed to excellence.
